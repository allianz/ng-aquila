Additional type aliases

NxButtonType

Type of a button.

Type: 'primary' | 'secondary' | 'tertiary' | 'cta' | 'emphasis' | 'attention'

NxButtonSize

Type: 'small' | 'small-medium' | 'medium' | 'large' | 's' | 'm'

NxButtonColorScheme

Type: 'default' | 'accent-attention' | 'on-accent-attention'

NxButtonAccentColor

Accent color of a button. Only takes effect when colorScheme is accent-attention or on-accent-attention. Only relevant for the A1 Design.

Type: 'yellow' | 'orange' | 'red' | 'purple' | 'aqua' | 'blue' | 'teal' | 'green' | 'gray'

NxPlainButtonSize

Please note: small is only meant for the A1 Design.

s and m are the A1 shirt sizes and are treated as aliases of small and medium respectively.

Type: 'medium' | 'small' | 's' | 'm'

NxPlainButtonVariant

Please note: secondary is only for meant for the A1 Design

Type: 'primary' | 'secondary'

NxPlainButtonColorScheme

Color scheme of a plain button. Only relevant for the A1 Design.

The plain button has no accent colors.

  • default – the regular action-colored plain button
  • on-accent-attention – for use on an accent-attention surface
  • on-brand – for use on a brand-colored surface

Type: 'default' | 'on-accent-attention' | 'on-brand'
